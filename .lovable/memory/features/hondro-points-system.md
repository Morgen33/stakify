---
name: Hondro Points System
description: Universal platform reward currency with burn mechanics, master minting, and consumer purchasing
type: feature
---
- Hondro Points = dev's way of giving back to the community using the platform
- Only the Master can mint/create new points (unlimited supply, controlled)
- Points are burned on use — when spent in marketplaces, trait store, etc., they're permanently destroyed
- Consumers can buy points (pricing TBD)
- Start with smaller reward amounts at launch to prevent inflation
- Points will integrate across all future platform modules (Trade Store, Trait Store, Arcade, etc.)
- Points are NOT a financial asset — they're a platform utility currency
- Burn-on-use model creates natural scarcity over time
